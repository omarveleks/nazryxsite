## A. What we do, close up (copy for /manufacturers and /partners)

### One line (hero of the manufacturers page)
**Your export team for frontier markets.**
We read each market line by line, show you where your products fit, and run the launch in your name: registration, importers, doctors and state buyers.

### What we do (three blocks, used on home and /manufacturers)

**1. Read the market.**
- We read each market's national register, pharmacy shelves and state purchase lists, product by product, for your whole catalogue.
- You see what is open, what is crowded, which state buyers need what, and where your price lands, before anyone signs anything.

**2. Register and launch.**
- We choose the route, prepare the local part of the dossier and translate it in-house.
- We follow the filing through the regulator and the plant inspection.
- We act as your local representative, appoint the importer and get you listed with the pharmacy chains.
- The registrations are always in your name.

**3. Sell and report.**
- Medical representatives call on the doctors who prescribe your products. Pharmacists are trained on the over-the-counter lines.
- We bid for you in the state rounds.
- You get a written report every week: orders, stock, listings, what to push and what to fix.

**For distributors (used on /partners):**
- We run your sourcing and market-intelligence function: suppliers found and vetted, quotes compared, registrations watched, and a weekly report.
- Run by people, not sold as a login.

### Why Nazryx (six points; use as a grid on home and /manufacturers)

1. **Evidence before contracts.** Your first piece of work from us is a market read, not an invoice. In Kazakhstan alone we track 1,340 pharmacies SKU by SKU and read every lot of the national state round.
2. **The market stays yours.** Registrations are held in your name. We run the market for you, and you can replace the importer without losing anything.
3. **Paid on results.** We earn commission on sales, agreed per market. No fee for introductions.
4. **On the ground.** We have teams in Lahore and Almaty. In Kazakhstan that means 1,000+ physicians in our network, 100+ distributor relationships, and Kazakh and Russian translation done in-house.
5. **Selective.** We represent 3 manufacturers in Kazakhstan and 10 worldwide, in selected, exclusive partnerships. Each one gets real regulatory and commercial work in the market.
6. **Built for exporters from Pakistan, Bangladesh, Turkey, Egypt, Europe and Korea.** We know where your price lands against Indian, Western and local brands, and we build the plan around that edge.

### How it starts (4 steps; keep the original timeline section and correct it to this)
1. **A 20-minute call** about your portfolio, certificates and target markets.
2. **The market read, 2–3 weeks.** Your catalogue against the register, shelves and state buyers of each target market.
3. **The proposal:** products, filing waves, registration route, launch plan and volumes by year.
4. **Filing and launch.** First sales typically 12–24 months after filing, depending on the market and route.

## B. Facts for each market (checked 11 Nov 2026)

Use exact figures with their year. If a figure isn't listed here, it doesn't go on the site.

### Kazakhstan (shown in full on `/markets/kazakhstan`)
- Medicines market 2025: **USD 2.5bn**, retail and hospital. **85%** of value is imported. **9,659** pharmacies.
- State buyer: **SK-Pharmacy**, one national round. 2027 round: **976 lots, about USD 860m**.
- Registration:
  - national route open until **31 Dec 2027**
  - EAEU route **140 working days** plus clock stops, extendable to Russia, Belarus, Armenia and Kyrgyzstan
- All other Kazakhstan facts are in the round-1 brief, section 1.

### Uzbekistan
- Population: **38.2 million** (1 Jan 2026).
- Pharmaceutical turnover 2025: **about USD 2bn, up 18%** on 2024.
- Medicine imports 2025: **about USD 2bn**. The government is pushing to cut them through local production. (kun.uz, 27 Feb 2026)
- Registration was reformed in Nov 2025. Review time by safety category:
  - **60 days** for the lowest-risk category, down from 150
  - **90 days** for category 2
  - **120 days** for the higher-risk categories
  - Generics get a shortened registration period. (kun.uz, 5 Nov 2025)

### Tanzania
- Imports **more than 80%** of its medicines and medical devices. Local output covers **10–20%** of demand.
- Import bill: **about USD 1bn a year**.
- State buyer: **Medical Stores Department (MSD)**, which buys **about USD 491m** of health commodities a year for **8,800+** public facilities. (Daily News, 28 Sep 2026)
- Regulator: **TMDA**.
  - Full-review target **180 days**.
  - Average approval time for generics fell from **202 days (2020) to 61 days (2022)**. (Frontiers in Medicine, 2024)
- Nazryx has read **9,270+** registered product records.

### Kenya
- About **70%** of medicines are imported.
- Pharmaceutical imports: **about USD 705m in 2024**.
- Local makers produce **220 of the 1,096** formulations on the national essential medicines list. (African Exponent, 9 Jun 2026)
- Regulator: **Pharmacy and Poisons Board**.
  - Full-review target **262 days**.
  - Verification review for products already approved by reference authorities: **90-day target**. (Frontiers in Medicine, 2024)
- State buyer: **KEMSA**.

### Uganda
- At least **70%** of medicines are imported. (US Commercial Service, Dec 2025)
- Medicament imports 2025: **about USD 700m**. (Business Times Uganda, 22 Jun 2026)
- **25** sites licensed to make medicines, **13** in commercial production. (US Commercial Service, Dec 2025)
- Regulator: **National Drug Authority (NDA)**.
  - Full-review target **261 days**.
  - Verification review **90-day target**. (Frontiers in Medicine, 2024)
- State buyer: **National Medical Stores**.

### East African Community (applies to Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania)
- **7** regulators share assessment work through the **EAC medicines registration harmonisation** programme.
- The **CTD format is mandatory** in all of them.
- **Abridged and fast-track reviews** are available everywhere.
- Suggested line: "One dossier format across East Africa." (Frontiers in Medicine, 2024)

### Iraq
- Local production covers **more than 40%** of national demand, from about **40** factories. The rest is imported. (Iraqi Ministry of Health via Shafaq News, 23 Nov 2025)
- Imported medicines: **about USD 3bn a year**, the government's own figure. (Prime Minister Al-Sudani via Shafaq News, 13 Jul 2024)
- State buyer: **Kimadia**, the state company that imports medicines for the Ministry of Health.
- Kimadia's allocation is **1.66 trillion dinars** (Shafaq News, 22 Jun 2024). On the site, say "a single state importer" and do not show the amount, because the figure is dated.

### Sudan
- Imported **more than USD 274m** of medicines in the first half of 2026.
- **13** pharmaceutical factories have resumed operations.
- The National Fund for Medical Supplies reports availability **above 70%**. (North Africa Post, 4 Aug 2026)
- Wording rule: positive and factual ("a market rebuilding its supply, buying through one national fund"). Never mention the war.
