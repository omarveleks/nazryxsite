06 · Where we work
Where we work

14 markets, 3 continents. Each one its own game.

Central Asia and the Caucasus

One dossier, five markets.

Kazakhstan · Uzbekistan · Azerbaijan

With Kazakhstan as EAEU reference state, one registration can be extended to Russia, Belarus, Armenia and Kyrgyzstan. The national route closes on 31 Dec 2027.

East Africa

A good dossier now travels.

Kenya · Uganda · Tanzania · Rwanda

Seven East African regulators share assessment work and the CTD format is mandatory. Done right once, a dossier opens the region.

West Africa

Import-led, centrally bought.

Nigeria · Ghana · Senegal · Francophone West and Central Africa

Much of the volume runs through central state buyers. Winning here is about registration and price, not presence.

North Africa and the Horn

Supply is being rebuilt.

Sudan · Libya

National buyers are rebuilding supply and need reliable makers. Early, compliant suppliers earn the position.

Middle East

One buyer decides.

Iraq

A single state importer sets the volume. The strategy is to be registered, priced and ready when its lists open.

South America

Price-led, centrally bought.

Peru

A central state buyer and a price-led market, where Indian generics already lead at about 14.5% of supply.
