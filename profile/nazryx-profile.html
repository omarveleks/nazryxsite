05 · Our story
Our story

Every tablet has a journey. We make it work.

A single tablet in a patient's hands in a village has a journey worth telling. It starts in a plant, crosses borders, passes a regulator, an importer and a pharmacy shelf, and only then reaches the person who needs it.

Manufacturers can't see where they fit. Buyers can't find reliable supply. Nazryx exists to close that gap.

07 · How we work
How we work

Four steps, from the first read to steady reorders.

1. Read

We read your portfolio against each market: what's open, thin or crowded, and in what order to launch.

2. Register

We choose the route, complete the local dossier and follow the filing through, in your name.

3. Launch

We vet and appoint the importer, secure pharmacy chain listings and prepare state bids.

4. Grow

We stay on the account: doctors, prices, state rounds, reorders, and a written report every week.
