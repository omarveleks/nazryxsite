Why it works

A supplier list tells you who exists. It doesn't tell you what's open.

Most sourcing and export decisions get made from catalogues, brochures and whoever answered the email — because those are the things that are easy to get hold of. What actually decides whether a product will sell sits in the national registers, and it only gives anything up when the whole thing is read rather than searched.

53 registrations. All veterinary.

A widely used molecule came back as saturated — on paper, a category nobody should enter. Every registration was for animal use. There was no human-medicine supplier in the country at all, against a serious disease burden.

Twelve products. No supplier.

An entire clinical category with nothing registered against it in a market of sixty million people — and a distributor there who had asked us for one of those twelve by name, weeks before we found the rest.

Nazir & Company International (PVT) Limited

Nazryx is a trade agency and data facilitation platform focused on market research, data analysis and lead generation within the pharmaceutical industry.

It is a subsidiary of Nazir & Company (PVT) Limited, a multinational construction company spanning Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, Libya, Iraq and the Middle East, with offices across the world. PEC License: 00001 (C-A / No Limit).

Nazryx was created as a result of deep research across pharmaceutical sectors, where a lack of efficient sales structures and practices was identified — weak data usage, slow lead generation and inefficient CRMs among them.

Nazryx aims to bridge that gap using proprietary software, conducting research and training for companies, and developing market access platforms for SME pharmaceutical companies.
