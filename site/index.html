The gap

Frontier markets import most of their medicines. 70–95% in ours.

Their data is scattered and every market has its own rules. Manufacturers can't see where they fit; buyers can't find reliable supply. Import-led markets buy on price, supply and registration. That is the work we do.

Why it works

A supplier list tells you who exists. It doesn't tell you what's open.

Most sourcing and export decisions get made from catalogues, brochures and whoever answered the email — because those are the things that are easy to get hold of. What actually decides whether a product will sell sits in the national registers, and it only gives anything up when the whole thing is read rather than searched.

53 registrations. All veterinary.

A widely used molecule came back as saturated — on paper, a category nobody should enter. Every registration was for animal use. There was no human-medicine supplier in the country at all, against a serious disease burden.

Twelve products. No supplier.

An entire clinical category with nothing registered against it in one of our markets — and a distributor there who had asked us for one of those twelve by name, weeks before we found the rest.
