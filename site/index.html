Why now

The rules are changing. Early movers win.

Africa is converging

The African Medicines Agency became operational in October 2025. East Africa already shares assessments across 7 regulators.

Eurasia is consolidating

National registrations not aligned to EAEU rules are being annulled in several member states. Kazakhstan's national route closes on 31 Dec 2027.

South Asia is exporting

Pakistan's pharmaceutical exports hit a 20-year high in FY2025. Bangladesh now exports to more than 130 countries.
