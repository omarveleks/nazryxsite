The problem

Most export launches fail before the first shipment.

Not because of the product or the price. Because of what was decided before anyone read the market.

01

The wrong market

Chosen from a brochure, not from the national register. Crowded categories look open; open ones go unnoticed.

02

The wrong partner

Picked because they replied first, not because they sell your category or reach the right buyers.

03

Registration found too late

The route is worked out after the product is chosen. A year and a dossier are lost to dead ends.

Questions

What manufacturers ask first.

Are you an agent, a consultant or a distributor?

An agent. We never take title to stock. We read the market, register your products, appoint the importer, run promotion and state bids, and report weekly.

How are you paid?

On results: commission on sales, agreed per market before anything starts. There is no fee for introductions.

Whose name is on the registration?

Yours. Registrations are always held in the manufacturer's name. We act as your local representative and are never the holder.

How long until first sales?

Typically 12–24 months after filing, depending on the market and the route.

What do you need from us to start?

Your product list with dosage forms and strengths, and your certificates. Dossiers come later, and only for the products you take forward.
