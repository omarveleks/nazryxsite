What we run

Eight functions, operated continuously.

Each of these exists inside most pharmaceutical companies already, done partially, by someone with another job. We take them over as a set — because they only compound when they share the same underlying data.

01

Market and demand intelligence

Demand patterns, competitive positioning and regulatory trends studied per molecule, per market — so a product enters a country because the evidence supports it, not because it was available.

02

Manufacturer discovery and vetting

We search manufacturer portfolios across markets by molecule, dosage form, capability, certification and market presence — then assess who is actually credible before a name reaches you.

03

Portfolio consolidation

Finding one manufacturer for one product is easy. Finding the one who can cover eight of your lines is the work — and it is where the savings actually sit.

04

Registration and regulatory tracking

Live registration counts per presentation, pathway assessment and realistic timelines — monitored, so a lapse in your own portfolio or an opening in someone else's is something you hear about rather than discover.

05

Pricing and RFQ workflow

Once a shortlist is right, we run the quotation round. You receive a comparison, not a stack of unfiltered emails to work through.

06

Buyer and distributor analytics

Your existing customer base is usually the most underused asset in the business. We work it as data: what to offer whom, where promotion pays, and which accounts are quietly declining.

07

Your private portal

Everything above lands in one place, built around your catalogue and shared across your team — so the analysis does not live in one person's inbox. It updates continuously and acts as a team-wide brain rather than a monthly report nobody opens.

08

Quarterly growth reviews

Every quarter we sit down with your team and go through what moved, what did not and why — then reset the targets. With our analysts, not a dashboard export.

Onboarding

About a week, and one person from your side.

The model is only as good as what it knows about you, so the first week is spent loading your business into it properly. After that the work runs without pulling on your team.

Before we start
A scoping call

Twenty minutes on your categories, your markets and where the friction actually is. If we are not the right fit we will say so on that call rather than after an invoice.

Days 1–3
We load your business

Existing catalogue, supplier and buyer lists, category preferences, target markets and current goals. This is the part that needs your point of contact; most of it is exports you already have.

Days 4–7
We tune the model and build your portal

Scoring weighted to your economics, not a generic template. A distributor optimising cash flow and a manufacturer chasing registrations need different answers from the same data.

Week 2 onward
The function runs

Weekly reporting, live portal, opportunities and alerts as they are found, RFQs on request. Quarterly reviews with our team to reset direction.

Questions

The things people ask on the first call.

Is this software, or people?

Both, and the order matters. We built the software because the analysis is not possible by hand at this scale. But you are not buying a login — our team operates it, and what reaches you is worked output rather than a screen you have to interpret.

The portal exists so your team can see and use that output. It is not the product.

What do you need from us, realistically?

One person, for about a week, at onboarding. Your product catalogue, supplier list, the markets you sell into and your category priorities. Most of it is exports you already have; none of it needs to be tidy first.

After onboarding, the demand on your side drops sharply — a weekly read and a call when something needs a decision.

How is this different from a market research report?

A report describes a market on the day it was written and then starts ageing. Registers change, suppliers exit, registrations lapse, tenders open.

We are not producing a document. We are watching a specific catalogue against live sources and telling you when something moves — and then doing the sourcing and quotation work that follows.

Do you replace our sales team?

No, and we would be suspicious of anyone claiming to. We take the research, qualification and comparison work off them so they spend their time in front of buyers instead of in spreadsheets.

What happens to our data?

Your catalogue, supplier lists and buyer data are used for your partnership and nothing else. They are not pooled, resold, or used to advise anyone else. Terms are written into the agreement rather than left to trust.

Which markets do you cover?

We work across 14 markets on 3 continents: Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Azerbaijan, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Nigeria, Ghana, Senegal, francophone West and Central Africa, Sudan, Libya, Iraq and Peru. Teams in Lahore and on the ground in Almaty. Sourcing runs through Pakistan, Bangladesh, Turkey, Egypt, Europe and Korea.

If your market is not one we cover, we will tell you on the first call.

Why is intake limited?

Because the model is tuned per catalogue and the work is done by people. A partnership that is spread thin is not worth having on either side, so we hold a small number at a time and take on new ones when there is genuine capacity.

Start with twenty minutes.

Tell us what you carry and where you sell it. We will tell you what we would look at first, and whether there is enough there to be worth either of our time. No preparation needed on your side.
