What we run

Eight functions, run every week.

Most companies do these in the gaps between other work. We take them over as a set.

01

Market and demand intelligence

Demand, competition and registrations, per molecule and per market. Products enter where the evidence supports them.

02

Manufacturer discovery and vetting

Manufacturers searched by molecule, dosage form and certification. Only credible ones reach you.

03

Portfolio consolidation

The fewest manufacturers that cover the most of your lines. Fewer audits, simpler shipments.

04

Registration tracking

Registration position per molecule and market. Lapses and new entrants flagged as they happen.

05

Pricing and RFQs

Quotation rounds run for you. Quotes compared like for like.

06

Buyer analytics

Your own customers as data: what to offer whom, and which accounts are slowing.

07

Questions

What distributors ask first.

Is this software, or people?

People, using our own software. You are not buying a login: our team does the work and you get the result, weekly.

What do you need from us?

One person for about a week at the start: your catalogue, supplier list, markets and priorities. After that, a weekly read and a call when a decision is needed.

What happens to our data?

It is used for your partnership only. Not pooled, not resold, not used for anyone else. This is written into the agreement.

Which markets do you cover?

Kazakhstan, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Sudan, Iraq and Uzbekistan, sourcing from Pakistan, Bangladesh, Turkey, Egypt, Europe and South Korea.
