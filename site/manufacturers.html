Questions

What manufacturers ask first.

Are you an agent, a consultant or a distributor?

An agent. We never take title to stock. We register your products, appoint the importer, run promotion and state bids, and report weekly.

How are you paid?

On results: commission on sales, agreed per market before anything starts. There is no fee for introductions.

Whose name is on the registration?

Yours. Registrations are always held in the manufacturer's name. We act as your local representative and are never the holder. You keep the product, dossier, plant and brand.

Do we need an EAEU GMP certificate?

It depends on the route. We check your plant's position for the national and the EAEU route on the first call, before you spend anything on a dossier.

We already have a distributor in that market.

Then we look at whether they are the right one. Often the distributor is fine and the gap is pricing, promotion or a state bid. The importer can always be replaced, because the registration is in your name.

How long until first sales?

12–24 months after filing, depending on the route. The national route can bring sales in 2028; the EAEU route in 2029.

What do you need from us to start?

Your product list with dosage forms and strengths, and your certification pack. Dossiers come later, and only for the products you take forward.
