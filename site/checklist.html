The checklist

Frontier market entry: ten questions

  1. Is the market chosen on evidence?Competition, price and demand for your products, not a brochure or a first reply.
  2. Which of your lines have room?Lead with the products that face the least competition, not the ones you sell most at home.
  3. Which registration route, and how long?National or regional, and the real clock including questions and the plant inspection.
  4. Is your dossier ready for that route?Format, translations and the local parts, completed only for the products you will file.
  5. Is your plant ready for inspection?Some routes inspect the site before any product is approved.
  6. Whose name is on the registration?It should be yours, so the market stays yours if a partner changes.
  7. Who will actually sell it?Partners judged by what they really carry and who they reach.
  8. Where does your price land?Against the products already on the shelf, not against a target margin.
  9. Who buys in volume?State buyers, hospitals and pharmacy chains, and when their rounds open.
  10. How will you know it's working?Agree weekly reporting on orders, stock and listings before launch.
