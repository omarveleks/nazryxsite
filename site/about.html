Our story

From the plant to the patient.

A single tablet in a patient's hands in a village has a journey worth telling. It starts in a plant, crosses borders, passes a regulator, an importer and a pharmacy shelf, and only then reaches the person who needs it.

Frontier markets import most of their medicines, yet their data is scattered and every country has its own rules. Manufacturers can't see where they fit. Buyers can't find reliable supply.

Nazryx exists to close that gap. We read these markets line by line, and we take manufacturers into them, with the registrations in their name.
