Who buys

Chains, hospitals and one state buyer.

Pharmacy chains

Chains hold 76–77% of pharmacy turnover. The top 15 run 2,507 pharmacies and hold 50.5%.

The state buyer

SK-Pharmacy buys for free and insured care in one national round a year. 2027 round: 976 lots, about USD 860m.

Priority rules

Local and EAEU producers have priority in the state round. We position you where there is a way in.
