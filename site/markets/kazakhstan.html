Pharmacies and chains

76–77% of pharmacy turnover goes through chains. The top 15 chains run 2,507 pharmacies and hold 50.5%.

The state buyer

SK-Pharmacy buys for free and insured care in one national round a year. 2027 round: 976 lots, about USD 860m. Local and EAEU producers have priority.
