Questions

What distributors ask first.

Is this software, or people?

People, using our own software. You are not buying a login: our team does the work and you get the result, weekly.

What do you need from us?

One person for about a week at the start: your catalogue, supplier list, markets and priorities. After that, a weekly read and a call when a decision is needed.

What happens to our data?

It is used for your partnership only. Not pooled, not resold, not used for anyone else. This is written into the agreement.

Start with twenty minutes.

Tell us what you carry and where you sell it. We will tell you what we would look at first. We reply within one working day.
